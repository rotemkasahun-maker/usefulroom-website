Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

This policy describes the public UsefulRoom informational website. Product-specific Google Sheets / Apps Script permissions and processing are still being reviewed and will be documented before a premium Google-connected product is publicly released.

Website data

This static website does not include a contact form, user account system, or our own analytics scripts. The hosting provider may process standard technical information such as IP addresses and request logs to serve and secure the site under its own policies.

Google Sheets products

Some UsefulRoom products are spreadsheets. Proposed premium interfaces may request Google permissions to read or update spreadsheet data. Those permissions are not required to browse this website. We will publish a verified product-specific explanation of requested scopes, data handling, and retention before public distribution of any Google-connected application. We do not claim that these applications have been verified by Google.

External services

Purchases, downloads, and Google services, where applicable, are provided through third-party platforms subject to their respective policies. No purchase or Google account connection is available directly through this website at this time.

Changes

We will update this page as services and product functionality change. Do not treat this provisional policy as an assurance that untested scripts have completed a security review.

Questions

For support or privacy questions, see our support page. A verified support contact address will be added before the public OAuth verification submission.
