Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

This policy covers the UsefulRoom informational website and the Home Bakery Profit Guard Google Sheets Premium product. Google OAuth verification is in progress; this page does not claim Google approval.

Website data

This static website has no UsefulRoom contact form, user account system, or UsefulRoom analytics scripts. The hosting provider may process standard technical information such as IP addresses and request logs to serve and secure the site under its own policies. The website does not request Google account access merely because someone visits it.

Google data accessed

When a user installs and authorizes the bound Apps Script in a Google Sheets copy, the script uses the Google Sheets permission to read and update the spreadsheet used by that copy. The implementation reads and writes the Settings, Ingredients, Recipes, Recipe Items, Quote Builder, and Orders tabs for pricing and order calculations. The OAuth Sheets permission is broad at the Google API level; the implementation does not use it to search unrelated workbooks. The product does not request Gmail, Contacts, Calendar, or Drive-wide permissions.

How Google data is used

The spreadsheet values are used to calculate ingredient costs, recipe costs, target prices, modeled profit and margin, pricing guardrails, and open-order totals, and to display those results in the dashboard. When the user invokes a dashboard save action, entered ingredient prices or quote values are written back to the user's spreadsheet. The data is not used for advertising, profiling, credit decisions, or unrelated product development.

Sharing, transfer, and disclosure

The Apps Script does not send spreadsheet contents to UsefulRoom servers, advertising networks, data brokers, or other external APIs. Google processes the data through Google Sheets and Apps Script under Google's terms and privacy policy. The user controls sharing of the spreadsheet through Google Drive. UsefulRoom does not sell or rent Google user data. Marketplace support messages, if used, are processed through the marketplace's systems and policies; users should not include spreadsheet contents in a support message.

Protection and security

Access to the spreadsheet remains subject to the user's Google authorization and Google Drive sharing settings. The script is bound to the user's spreadsheet copy and does not store a master-workbook ID or collect Google passwords, OAuth tokens, or payment-card data. The product uses Google's authorization controls and the HTTPS Google Sheets/Apps Script environment. Users should share the workbook only with trusted collaborators and revoke the script's access in their Google Account when they stop using it.

Retention and deletion

UsefulRoom does not maintain a separate database or retained copy of the spreadsheet data. The user can delete values or the workbook in Google Sheets and revoke the script's authorization through Google Account security settings. Google’s own retention and deletion controls apply to data remaining in the user’s Google account. Any marketplace support-message retention is controlled by that marketplace; UsefulRoom does not copy spreadsheet contents into a separate support store.

Privacy questions

A dedicated UsefulRoom support email is not currently configured. Until one is published, privacy questions should be sent through the marketplace order-support channel described on the support page. Do not include spreadsheet contents, passwords, OAuth tokens, or other sensitive data in a support request.

Changes

We will update this policy if the website, product data flow, permissions, or support process changes.
